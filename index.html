Reading without a server

On the first screen, tap Try a sample comic (Captain Science #2, 1951, public domain) or Just read on this device. Bring in CBZ, CBR or PDF files from the Files app, AirDrop, or another app's Share menu. They stay on your device.

Reading from a Longbox server

Longbox can also read from a Longbox server: a small program that runs on your own Mac or PC and shares your comics folder with your devices. Enter its address on the first screen and pick your profile.

Common questions

Does Longbox come with comics? Only the public-domain sample. Longbox reads the comics you already have.

Where's my reading kept? On your device, and on your Longbox server if you use one. See the privacy policy.
