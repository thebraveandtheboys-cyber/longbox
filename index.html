Questions, bugs, or something on Longbox Social we should see. We answer within a couple of days, and reports within 24 hours.

Reading without a server

On the first screen, tap Try a sample comic (Captain Science #2, 1951, public domain) or Just read on this device. Bring in CBZ, CBR or PDF files from the Files app, AirDrop, or another app's Share menu. They stay on your device.

Reading from a Longbox server

Longbox can also read from a Longbox server: a small program that runs on your own Mac or PC and shares your comics folder with your devices. Enter its address on the first screen and pick your profile.

Longbox Social

An optional public diary: what you read, your stars and reviews, people to follow, comments and lists. Join from the Social tab with Sign in with Apple or your email. Everyone who joins agrees to the community rules.

  • Report anything from its ⋯ menu. Every report is looked at within 24 hours.
  • Block someone from their page. Neither of you sees the other.
  • Delete your account any time: Social, then Account, then Delete my account. Everything you posted goes with it.

Common questions

Does Longbox come with comics? Only the public-domain sample. Longbox reads the comics you already have.

Where's my reading kept? On your device, and on your Longbox server if you use one. Only what you choose to share goes to Longbox Social. See the privacy policy.
