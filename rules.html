Community rules

Longbox Social is people talking about comics. Everyone agrees to these before they can post.

  • No harassment, threats, hate or slurs, about anyone.
  • No sexual content, and nothing sexual involving minors, ever.
  • No spam, scams, ads, or links to pirated comics.
  • Don't post anyone's private information.
  • Mark spoilers as spoilers.
  • Post only words you have the right to share.

There's no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive people. Posts that break these rules are removed, and people who break them are banned.

How we keep to them

  • Every post, comment and person can be reported from its ⋯ menu. Reports reach the people who run Longbox right away, and every one is looked at within 24 hours.
  • Anyone can block anyone. Blocked people can't see your posts, comment on them, or follow you, and you don't see theirs.
  • Slurs and the worst words are filtered out of names, comments and lists, and starred out of reviews.
