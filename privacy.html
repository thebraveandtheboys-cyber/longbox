Privacy policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

Longbox is a comic reader. It has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, and we don't sell or share anything about you. This page says what Longbox keeps, where, and who can see it.

On your device

Comics you bring in, where you are in each book, your journal (dates, stars, reviews), reading stats, reading lists and settings are kept on your iPhone or iPad. They don't leave it unless you use a Longbox server or Longbox Social, below. Deleting the app deletes them.

Your Longbox server, if you use one

A Longbox server is a program you (or a friend) run on your own computer. When you connect to one, your profile's reading progress, journal, lists and settings are kept on that server, so your devices stay in step. We don't run these servers and can't see them. The person who runs a server can see what's on it.

To show covers and details for books, a Longbox server may look books up on Metron, a public comics database, and the app loads Metron's cover images directly. Metron sees those requests the way any website does.

Longbox Social, if you join

Longbox Social is optional. It's run on Supabase, a hosted database service. When you join, it keeps:

  • Your sign-in: your email address, or the identifier Sign in with Apple gives (which can be a private relay address). Never shown to anyone.
  • Your profile: username, display name, bio and Top Four. Public.
  • Your diary: the books you log, with the date, stars and review. Public, except entries you set to Just me, which only you can see.
  • What you do there: likes, comments, lists, follows and Want to read. Public, except lists and Want to read entries you keep to yourself.
  • Blocks and reports: who you've blocked, and reports you file. Only you can see your blocks. Reports are seen by you and the people who run Longbox.

Nothing from your journal is posted unless Post what I read is on (Social, then Account), and only entries you share with friends, from the day you joined, unless you ask to post earlier ones.

The database itself enforces who can see what, so a private entry stays private even outside the app.

Deleting your data

  • Longbox Social: Social, then Account, then Delete my account. Your profile, diary, reviews, comments, likes, lists and follows are deleted right away.
  • Your device: delete the app.
  • A Longbox server: ask whoever runs it, or delete your profile there.

Or email us and we'll do it for you.

Children

Longbox Social isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly keep their information. If you think a child has joined, email us and we'll delete the account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes here with a new date. Big changes will be pointed out in the app.
